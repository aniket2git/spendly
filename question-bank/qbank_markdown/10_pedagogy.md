# PART 11 - Teaching Methodology and Child Pedagogy

> *Weightage: 10 to 20 marks in DSSSB, 10 to 25 across KVS / NVS / EMRS. The highest marks-per-hour in the entire syllabus, and the section most candidates under-prepare.*



## 11.1 Child Development



> **WEIGHTAGE: 3-6 marks | Newborn abilities and development principles recur | Priority CRITICAL**



### 11.1.1 Questions



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] Which of the following distinguishes growth from development?**

- (a) They are identical
- (b) **Growth is quantitative and stops at maturity; development is qualitative and continues lifelong  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Growth is qualitative
- (d) Development stops at adolescence

> **Why:** **Growth** = measurable increase in size, weight, vocabulary count - and it **stops**. **Development** = qualitative improvement in functioning and organisation - and it continues "womb to tomb".



**Q. [EXPECTED] Development proceeding from head to toe is called:**

- (a) Proximodistal
- (b) **Cephalocaudal  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Bilateral
- (d) Distal

> **Why:** **Cephalocaudal** = head to toe (a baby controls its head before its legs). **Proximodistal** = centre outward (shoulder and arm control before finger control).



**Q. [PYQ] Auditory and visual co-ordination system is present in child from ______.**

- (a) **Birth  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) 3 weeks of birth
- (c) 6 days of birth
- (d) 6 months of birth

> **Why:** A newborn turns its head and eyes toward a sound - an **innate**, not learned, ability. Note that **vision is the weakest sense at birth** (acuity roughly 20/400, focal distance 8-12 inches) while **touch is the most mature**.



**Q. [PYQ] [NUMERICAL] Regarding vision of new-born babies: I. Neonates can discriminate stationary black and white stripes 1/8 inch wide. II. By three months babies can see stripes as narrow as 1/64 inch.**

- (a) **Both I and II  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Neither I nor II
- (c) Only I
- (d) Only II

> **Why:** Both are standard infant-acuity findings. Learn the pair of fractions - **1/8 inch at birth, 1/64 inch by three months** - and note the direction: as vision sharpens, the denominator grows.



**Q. [PYQ] Broca's area and Wernicke's area are concerned with language. These are the areas of: I. right hemisphere. II. left hemisphere.**

- (a) **Only II  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Only I
- (c) Both I and II
- (d) Neither I nor II

> **Why:** Language is **lateralised to the LEFT hemisphere** in about 95% of right-handed people. **Broca's area** (frontal lobe) handles speech **production**; **Wernicke's area** (temporal lobe) handles **comprehension**. Hook: **L**eft for **L**anguage and **L**ogic.



**Q. [PYQ] Which of the following ailments is NOT caused by obesity?**

- (a) High blood pressure
- (b) **AIDS  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Orthopedic problem
- (d) Diabetes

> **Why:** **AIDS is an infectious disease caused by the HIV virus**; obesity is a metabolic condition. Obesity genuinely causes hypertension, type 2 diabetes, orthopaedic problems and sleep apnoea.



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] Which deficiency causes night blindness?**

- (a) Vitamin C
- (b) Vitamin D
- (c) **Vitamin A  <-- CORRECT**
- (d) Iron

> **Why:** **Vitamin A** = night blindness; **Vitamin C** = scurvy; **Vitamin D** = rickets; **Iodine** = goitre; **Vitamin B1** = beri-beri; **Iron** = anaemia (very common among Indian schoolchildren).



## 11.2 Theories of Development



> **WEIGHTAGE: 3-6 marks | Piaget, Erikson, Marcia and Kohlberg are all fair game | Priority CRITICAL**



### 11.2.1 Questions



**Q. [PYQ] Piaget's formal operational stage begins at approximately ______.**

- (a) **11 years  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) 14 years
- (c) 8 years
- (d) 7 years

> **Why:** Learn the boundary chain **0 - 2 - 7 - 11**: Sensorimotor 0-2, Pre-operational 2-7, Concrete operational 7-11, **Formal operational 11 onwards** (abstract and hypothetical reasoning).



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] A child who cannot take another person's viewpoint is displaying:**

- (a) conservation
- (b) **egocentrism  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) reversibility
- (d) seriation

> **Why:** **Egocentrism** is characteristic of the **pre-operational** stage (2-7), along with **centration** (focusing on one feature only), animism and irreversibility. **Conservation** and **reversibility** are achieved later, in the concrete operational stage.



**Q. [EXPECTED] Object permanence develops during which Piagetian stage?**

- (a) **Sensorimotor  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Pre-operational
- (c) Concrete operational
- (d) Formal operational

> **Why:** Around **8 months**, the infant understands that a hidden object still exists. It marks the beginning of mental representation.



**Q. [PYQ] Inconsistency in the development of different type of conservation is called ______.**

- (a) **Horizontal Decalage  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Meta-cognition
- (c) Selective attention
- (d) Immersion

> **Why:** *Decalage* is French for "gap"; **horizontal** means within the same stage. So it is a gap **across tasks at one stage** - a child may conserve number but not yet volume. Distractors: **metacognition** = thinking about one's own thinking; **immersion** = a language-teaching approach.



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] The gap between what a learner can do alone and what they can do with guidance is called:**

- (a) scaffolding
- (b) **the Zone of Proximal Development  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) assimilation
- (d) equilibration

> **Why:** **ZPD** is the *gap* (Vygotsky); **scaffolding** is the temporary *support* provided within it, gradually withdrawn. Teaching should target the ZPD.



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] Which statement correctly contrasts Piaget and Vygotsky?**

- (a) Both saw development as purely biological
- (b) **Piaget held that development leads learning; Vygotsky held that learning leads development  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Vygotsky ignored language
- (d) Piaget emphasised culture more

> **Why:** **Piaget**: a solitary little scientist, universal biological stages, language follows thought. **Vygotsky**: a social apprentice, culture-dependent development, language drives thought.



**Q. [EXPECTED] In Erikson's theory, the crisis of adolescence (12-19 years) is:**

- (a) Trust vs Mistrust
- (b) Industry vs Inferiority
- (c) **Identity vs Role Confusion  <-- CORRECT**
- (d) Intimacy vs Isolation

> **Why:** Erikson gave **eight psychosocial stages** across the lifespan. Stage 4 (6-12) is Industry vs Inferiority; stage 5 is **Identity vs Role Confusion**; stage 6 (20-40) is Intimacy vs Isolation.



**Q. [PYQ] Which identity status is described by 'Experimenting and actively searching for alternatives in their struggle to establish their identity'?**

- (a) **Identity moratorium  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Identity diffusion
- (c) Identity foreclosure
- (d) Identity achievement

> **Why:** Exploration is underway but **no commitment yet** - and *moratorium* literally means a pause. Contrast: **diffusion** = neither exploring nor committed; **foreclosure** = committed **without** exploring (identity handed down by parents); **achievement** = explored **and** committed.



**Q. [EXPECTED] In Kohlberg's theory, obeying rules only to avoid punishment belongs to which level?**

- (a) **Pre-conventional  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Conventional
- (c) Post-conventional
- (d) Universal

> **Why:** **Pre-conventional** (up to about 9): punishment-obedience, then self-interest. **Conventional**: good-boy/good-girl approval, then law and order. **Post-conventional**: social contract, then universal ethical principles. What matters is the **reasoning**, not the choice.



**Q. [PYQ] [TRAP] Which of the following is NOT a Neo-Freudian Theory of Development? I. Sullivan's Interpersonal Theory. II. Erikson's Psycho-social Development.**

- (a) Only II
- (b) Both I and II
- (c) **Neither I nor II  <-- CORRECT**
- (d) Only I

> **Why:** A **double negative**. Both Sullivan and Erikson **ARE** Neo-Freudians, so neither qualifies as "not Neo-Freudian" - hence "Neither I nor II". Technique: first judge each statement's truth, *then* apply the question's negation. Neo-Freudians kept Freud's focus on the unconscious and early experience but rejected his emphasis on sexuality, stressing **social** factors instead.



**Q. [PYQ] [TRAP] Which is correctly matched? I. A child calling all four-legged animals doggie - under-generalization. II. A child using 'duck' only for his toy duck - over-generalizing.**

- (a) Both I and II
- (b) **Neither I nor II  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Only II
- (d) Only I

> **Why:** **Both labels are swapped.** Calling all animals "doggie" applies the word **too broadly = OVER-generalisation**. Using "duck" for only one toy applies it **too narrowly = UNDER-generalisation**. Hook: **OVER = too many things covered; UNDER = too few.**



**Q. [PYQ] In the context of Parenting, which of the following is correctly matched?**

- (a) Clearly communicating parent - Child being less liked
- (b) Power assertive parents - Coercive tactics in peer used by child
- (c) Parents being harsh - Unattractive social behavior of child
- (d) **Give and take relationship of parents - Children show same relationship with peer  <-- CORRECT**

> **Why:** Children **model** the interaction style they experience at home, so reciprocal give-and-take parenting (the **authoritative** style) produces children who are reciprocal with peers. Baumrind's four styles: **Authoritative** (high control + high warmth, best outcomes), **Authoritarian** (high control, low warmth), **Permissive** (low control, high warmth), **Neglectful** (low both, worst outcomes).



## 11.3 Learning, Teaching Methods and Lesson Planning



> **WEIGHTAGE: 3-6 marks | Herbartian steps and method-to-founder matching recur | Priority CRITICAL**



### 11.3.1 Questions



**Q. [PYQ] David Kolb's model of learning style is based on which type of learning?**

- (a) Inquiry-based learning
- (b) Integrative learning
- (c) Collaborative learning
- (d) **Experiential learning  <-- CORRECT**

> **Why:** Kolb defined learning as knowledge created through the **transformation of experience**. His four-stage cycle: **Concrete Experience, Reflective Observation, Abstract Conceptualisation, Active Experimentation**, giving four styles (Diverging, Assimilating, Converging, Accommodating).



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] Learning by suddenly perceiving the whole pattern - the "aha!" moment - was demonstrated by:**

- (a) Pavlov
- (b) Skinner
- (c) **Kohler  <-- CORRECT**
- (d) Thorndike

> **Why:** **Kohler** (Gestalt) with his chimpanzee experiments = **insight learning**. **Pavlov** = classical conditioning (association); **Skinner** = operant conditioning (consequences); **Thorndike** = trial and error with the Laws of Readiness, Exercise and **Effect**.



**Q. [EXPECTED] Learning by observing and imitating a model was proposed by:**

- (a) Piaget
- (b) **Bandura  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Ausubel
- (d) Maslow

> **Why:** **Bandura's** social/observational learning, famously the Bobo doll experiment. Its four processes: attention, retention, reproduction, motivation.



**Q. [PYQ] Heuristic method of teaching science was introduced by ______.**

- (a) William James
- (b) **Henry Edward Armstrong  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Lev Vygotsky
- (d) Abraham Maslow

> **Why:** **Armstrong**, an English chemist, argued students should learn science by **investigating like scientists** rather than being told. Remember the triple: **Armstrong - Heuristic - Science**. Compare **Kilpatrick - Project method** and **Dewey - learning by doing**.



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] Which teaching method moves from specific examples to a general rule?**

- (a) Deductive method
- (b) **Inductive method  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Lecture method
- (d) Demonstration method

> **Why:** **Inductive** = specific to general (discovering a formula from examples). **Deductive** = general rule to specific applications. Good practice often pairs them: induce the rule, then deduce applications.



**Q. [PYQ] When learning is meaning making, then students are the meaning makers. The learning process in this context is:**

- (a) **student-centred  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) teacher-centred
- (c) society-centred
- (d) subject-centred

> **Why:** If the **students** make the meaning, the process revolves around the students - the **constructivist** view, whose instructional form is learner-centred teaching with the teacher as **facilitator**.



**Q. [PYQ] [TRAP] ________ is a methodical approach. It suggests a logical and well-organised sequence of activities. It follows more definite steps.**

- (a) teaching strategy
- (b) teaching method
- (c) teaching technique
- (d) **teaching approach  <-- CORRECT**

> **Why:** Learn the hierarchy from broadest to narrowest: **Approach** (philosophy and assumptions) to **Method** (overall plan) to **Strategy** (plan for a specific goal) to **Technique** (the actual classroom move). "Methodical approach" plus "well-organised sequence" points to **approach**.



**Q. [PYQ] [TRAP] Which of the following is NOT one of the five steps of Herbartian lesson planning?**

- (a) Association
- (b) Presentation
- (c) Application
- (d) **Evaluation  <-- CORRECT**

> **Why:** Herbart's five: **Preparation, Presentation, Association/Comparison, Generalisation, Application.** **Evaluation** was added by later lesson-plan models, not by Herbart. Hook: "**P**lease **P**resent **A**nd **G**eneralise the **A**pplication."



**Q. [PYQ] For preparing a unit plan, which procedure is followed? I. Formation of proper units and sub-units if not prescribed in the syllabus. II. Determination of methods of assessment.**

- (a) **Both I and II  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Neither I nor II
- (c) Only I
- (d) Only II

> **Why:** Both are genuine steps: a unit plan must **define its boundaries** and must **specify how learning will be assessed**, because objectives and assessment must align. In "Both I and II" questions, check each statement independently and do not reject one merely for sounding obvious.



**Q. [PYQ] [TRAP] Which of the following statement is NOT correctly matched?**

- (a) Child with special need - should be seated near the board
- (b) **Performing art - every child should have clear view of board  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Group work - sitting arrangement should be in clump or clustered
- (d) Group discussion - teacher should have eye contact with every child

> **Why:** **Performing arts need open floor space** for movement and mutual observation - the blackboard is essentially irrelevant. The other three pairings are all correct.



**Q. [EXPECTED] Micro-teaching involves:**

- (a) teaching a full lesson to a large class
- (b) **practising one specific teaching skill with a small group for a short time, then getting feedback and re-teaching  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) teaching without any plan
- (d) teaching only gifted children

> **Why:** The cycle is **plan, teach, feedback, re-plan, re-teach, re-feedback**. It is a teacher-training technique for isolating and improving individual skills.



## 11.4 Assessment and Evaluation



> **WEIGHTAGE: 2-4 marks | The formative/summative/diagnostic distinction is near-certain | Priority CRITICAL**



### 11.4.1 Questions



**Q. [PYQ] Diagnostic assessment mainly helps in which of the following?**

- (a) Grading performance
- (b) Standardising content
- (c) Promoting learners
- (d) **Identifying misconceptions  <-- CORRECT**

> **Why:** *Diagnostic* comes from medicine - a diagnosis finds **what exactly is wrong** so the right remediation can follow. **Grading** and **promoting** are functions of **summative** assessment.



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] Assessment conducted during teaching to give feedback and adjust instruction is:**

- (a) summative
- (b) **formative  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) placement
- (d) prognostic

> **Why:** **Formative = assessment FOR learning** (during, continuous, feedback-driven). **Summative = assessment OF learning** (at the end, produces marks and grades). **Placement** decides the starting point; **diagnostic** locates specific difficulties.



**Q. [EXPECTED] CCE stands for:**

- (a) Common Curriculum Evaluation
- (b) **Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Cumulative Class Examination
- (d) Central Certification Exam

> **Why:** **Continuous** (throughout the year, not one final exam) and **Comprehensive** (both scholastic and co-scholastic areas - academics plus attitudes, values, health and arts).



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] A test that measures what it claims to measure is said to have:**

- (a) reliability
- (b) **validity  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) objectivity
- (d) usability

> **Why:** **Validity** = measures the right thing. **Reliability** = gives consistent results on repetition. **Objectivity** = different examiners award the same marks. Note the relationship: a test can be reliable **without** being valid, but it cannot be valid without being reliable.



**Q. [PYQ] All of the given are the methods of Information and Communication Technology-based assessment except ______.**

- (a) Concept map construction
- (b) **Writing reflection journal on paper  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Multimedia program development
- (d) Computer-based testing

> **Why:** The qualifier "**on paper**" removes all technology. A reflection journal *would* be ICT-based if kept as a blog or digital document - examiners insert such phrases deliberately, so read every option to the end.



**Q. [EXPECTED] In the revised Bloom's taxonomy, the highest cognitive level is:**

- (a) Evaluating
- (b) Analysing
- (c) Synthesising
- (d) **Creating  <-- CORRECT**

> **Why:** Revised (Anderson) order: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analysing, Evaluating, **Creating**. In the **original** taxonomy, Evaluation was highest and Synthesis came below it - so read which version is being asked.



**Q. [EXPECTED] A test that compares a student against a fixed learning standard rather than against classmates is:**

- (a) norm-referenced
- (b) **criterion-referenced  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) diagnostic
- (d) aptitude

> **Why:** **Criterion-referenced** measures against a defined outcome. **Norm-referenced** ranks the student against peers and deliberately spreads scores.



## 11.5 Inclusive Education, Policies and the Community



> **WEIGHTAGE: 3-6 marks | Act years and scheme target groups are pure recall | Priority CRITICAL**



### 11.5.1 Questions



**Q. [PYQ] The philosophy of inclusive education was added in District Primary Education Programme (DPEP) in ________ year.**

- (a) 1966
- (b) **1997  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) 2012
- (d) 1950

> **Why:** Sequence: **DPEP launched 1994**, the international **Salamanca Statement also 1994**, and India incorporated inclusive education into DPEP in **1997**.



**Q. [PYQ] [TRAP] Which law legally mandates the inclusion of children with disabilities into mainstream schools in India?**

- (a) **RPwD Act, 2016  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) National Curriculum Framework, 2005
- (c) RTI Act, 2009
- (d) NEP, 2020

> **Why:** Only the **RPwD Act 2016** is a **statute** mandating inclusion (it also recognises **21 disabilities**, with 4% job and 5% higher-education reservation). NCF 2005 is a *framework*; NEP 2020 is a *policy*. And note the trap: **RTI = Right to Information (2005)**, whereas the education law of 2009 is **RTE = Right to Education**.



**Q. [EXPECTED] The RTE Act 2009 guarantees free and compulsory education for children aged:**

- (a) 3 to 12 years
- (b) **6 to 14 years  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) 6 to 18 years
- (d) 0 to 6 years

> **Why:** The **86th Constitutional Amendment (2002)** inserted **Article 21A** making it a fundamental right; the RTE Act came into force on **1 April 2010**. It also mandates 25% reservation in private schools for disadvantaged groups and bans corporal punishment.



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] The key difference between integrated and inclusive education is:**

- (a) they are identical
- (b) **in integration the child adapts to the school; in inclusion the school adapts to the child  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) inclusion uses separate special schools
- (d) integration is only for gifted children

> **Why:** This one sentence is the whole distinction, and it is asked in many wordings. **Segregated** education places children with disabilities in entirely separate special schools.



**Q. [PYQ] Integrated child development service provides nutritious food for children who are ______.**

- (a) School going
- (b) School drop-out
- (c) **Below age of six years and too young to go to school  <-- CORRECT**
- (d) Girls below 12 years of age

> **Why:** **ICDS (launched 1975)** is an **early childhood (0-6)** programme delivered through **Anganwadi** centres, also covering pregnant and lactating mothers. School-going children are covered by the **Mid-Day Meal Scheme (PM POSHAN)** instead - keep the two separate.



**Q. [EXPECTED] The NEP 2020 school structure is:**

- (a) 10+2
- (b) **5+3+3+4  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) 8+4
- (d) 5+5+2

> **Why:** The **5+3+3+4** design: Foundational (5), Preparatory (3), Middle (3), Secondary (4). NEP 2020 also recommends the **mother tongue as medium up to at least Grade 5** and a target of **6% of GDP** for education.



**Q. [PYQ] Gender inequality refers to which of the following?**

- (a) **Social and economic disparities between genders  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Biological differences only
- (c) Differences in hairstyles
- (d) Uniform distribution of resources

> **Why:** **Sex is biological; gender is socially constructed.** Biological difference alone is not *inequality*, and "uniform distribution of resources" describes equality - the opposite of what is asked.



**Q. [PYQ] What is the core principle of the multilingual approach in early education?**

- (a) Prioritising grammar over communication
- (b) Replacing mother tongue with the target language quickly
- (c) **Welcoming the child's language and using it to build understanding  <-- CORRECT**
- (d) Teaching only in the national language

> **Why:** The home language is a **resource and a bridge**, not a problem to be removed. Rapid replacement is **submersion** ("sink or swim"), which harms both comprehension and self-esteem.



**Q. [PYQ] Aesthetic education primarily develops ______.**

- (a) speed in recall
- (b) logical reasoning
- (c) factual knowledge
- (d) **sensory responsiveness  <-- CORRECT**

> **Why:** Aesthetics is fundamentally about **perceiving and responding** to beauty, form, colour, sound and rhythm. Recall and factual knowledge are memory functions; logical reasoning is cognitive.



**Q. [PYQ] You are designing a curriculum for vocational education. To ensure learners can transfer skills to actual work settings, which principle should you emphasise most?**

- (a) **Principle of activity and practicality  <-- CORRECT**
- (b) Principle of articulation
- (c) Principle of recency
- (d) Principle of economy

> **Why:** Skills transfer to real workplaces only through **hands-on practice in realistic conditions**. *Articulation* concerns smooth progression between stages; *economy* concerns efficient use of time and resources.



**Q. [PYQ] In school, a 'subject' differs from a 'discipline' because it is ______.**

- (a) an external research field
- (b) more deeply organised
- (c) a philosophical field
- (d) **the branch of the discipline taught in the classroom  <-- CORRECT**

> **Why:** A **discipline** is the full scholarly domain produced by researchers; a **subject** is the curated, age-appropriate slice of it that appears on a timetable. School "Science" is a subject drawn from the disciplines of Physics, Chemistry and Biology.



**Q. [PYQ] Participation in which activities can enable the community to accept a teacher as one of them? I. Problems of unhygienic conditions. II. Problems of agriculture.**

- (a) Neither I nor II
- (b) Only I
- (c) **Both I and II  <-- CORRECT**
- (d) Only II

> **Why:** Acceptance comes from involvement in matters the community **actually cares about daily**. The wider principle: the teacher's role extends beyond the classroom into community service, so options restricting the teacher to purely academic work are usually wrong.



**Q. [PYQ] [TRAP] Which of the following is the activity related to community services?**

- (a) Collect picture about different musical instrument and dances
- (b) Collect information about handicrafts of different place
- (c) **Participating in decoration at the time of events  <-- CORRECT**
- (d) Explore food habit and living style of the people of different state

> **Why:** Apply one test: **is the student GIVING something to the community, or GETTING information from it?** Collecting pictures, information and habits all benefit the student's own learning - those are *project* work. Only decorating for a community event contributes effort **for the community's benefit**, which is service.



**Q. [EXPECTED] Action research is best described as:**

- (a) large-scale research by universities
- (b) **small-scale research by the teacher in their own classroom to solve an immediate practical problem  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) research on teaching methods by NCERT
- (d) a literature review

> **Why:** Its cycle is **plan, act, observe, reflect**. Related concept: **reflective teaching** - systematically examining what happened in a lesson and why, then changing practice.



**Q. [EXPECTED] [TRAP] Which of the following best describes the difference between guidance and counselling?**

- (a) They are identical
- (b) **Guidance is broader and more informational; counselling is a focused, confidential one-to-one professional relationship  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Counselling is only for academic problems
- (d) Guidance is only for adults

> **Why:** Guidance helps a person understand themselves and make informed choices (educational, vocational, personal). Counselling addresses a specific personal or psychological problem in confidence. The **cumulative record card** provides the basis for both.



**Q. [EXPECTED] Who among the following is known as Lokhitwadi?**

- (a) Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar
- (b) **Gopal Hari Deshmukh  <-- CORRECT**
- (c) Rabindranath Tagore
- (d) Ram Mohan Roy

> **Why:** Gopal Hari Deshmukh, the Marathi social reformer, wrote under the pen name **Lokhitwadi** ("one who desires the people's welfare"). Social-reformer questions appear in both the pedagogy and General Awareness sections.

> **First, circle the negative word.** A very large share of questions in these papers are framed as NOT, FALSE, EXCEPT or INCORRECT. Candidates who read fluently and answer the *positive* version of the question lose 5 to 10 marks per paper to nothing but haste. Physically mark that word on your rough sheet before you look at the options.
>
> **Second, eliminate before you guess.** You will not know every answer. But if you can rule out two options, the expected value of attempting is **+0.375** - clearly worth taking. If you can rule out nothing, leave it blank. That single discipline is often the difference between a score near the cut-off and one comfortably above it.
