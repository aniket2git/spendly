**An honest note on sources.** I have the three DSSSB papers this was built from, and I
have read the *published syllabi* of KVS, NVS and EMRS. I do **not** have the actual
question papers of KVS, NVS or any state TGT exam. So nothing here is labelled as being
"from the KVS paper" — that claim would be unverifiable. Every question is either a
genuine DSSSB PYQ or an honestly-marked expected question. The **concepts** are what
repeat across these exams, not the exact wordings.

## How to use it

1. Pick one chapter. **Cover the answers.** Attempt every question on paper.
2. Score with the real scheme: **+1** correct, **−0.25** wrong, **0** skipped.
3. Read **every** explanation — including for questions you got right. The explanations
   teach the *neighbouring* concepts, which is where the next paper's questions come from.
4. Keep a **leak list**: one line per wrong question, naming the *concept*, not the question.
5. That leak list is your revision syllabus. Nothing else.
6. Re-attempt after two weeks. Wrong twice = re-read the underlying theory.

## The two habits that decide the result

**Circle the negative word.** A large share of these questions are framed as NOT, FALSE,
EXCEPT or INCORRECT. Candidates lose 5–10 marks per paper to nothing but reading haste.

**Eliminate before you guess.** Rule out two of four options and attempting is worth
**+0.375** expected — clearly take it. Rule out nothing and leave it blank.
